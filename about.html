Our Story

How RADCON Was Born

Bob Daly and Cain Camilleri bring over 40 years of combined experience in steel fabrication and shipping container modification. After years of industry demand for a dedicated container modification specialist, RADCON was born.

Success in this business comes down to control: in-house manufacturing with stringent quality control, backed by our structural steel sister company, RAD Fabrication, and a licensed Professional Engineer on staff. We take ownership of every product we build — from design to fabrication to delivery.

After years with a large national company, Cain saw that real results come from listening to customers and controlling every stage of the work. Few competitors match RADCON's certifications and experience — which is why major businesses trust us to deliver on time and exceed expectations, again and again.
