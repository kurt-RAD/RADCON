Featured Case Study

Two-Story Burn Building — Nicholasville Fire Department, KY

The need: Nicholasville FD wanted to expand burn capabilities beyond single-story evolutions to reflect the challenges of modern residential fires.

The build: A container-based two-story burn building designed directly with the department's training staff — realistic layout for hose advancement and ventilation drills, engineered for expansion as future budgets allow.

The result, in the chief's words:

“The burn building has improved realism and effectiveness by expanding our burn capabilities to include a realistic two-story layout that creates challenges often found in modern homes. RADCON communicated throughout the process and delivered a great product from design to assembly.”

— Will Gover, Deputy Chief of Training/Safety, Nicholasville Fire Department
