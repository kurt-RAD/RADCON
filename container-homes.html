Container Home Questions

How much does a container home cost in Indiana?

A real, code-compliant, insulated home runs $150,000 to over $1 million depending on size, container count, glazing, and finish. Read our full cost & permit guide.

Are container homes legal in Indiana?

Yes — county by county. Indiana follows the IBC, which added container provisions in 2021. Our PE-stamped drawings are the document that turns a skeptical plan reviewer into a cooperative one.

How do you insulate for Indiana winters?

Batt or spray foam insulation, specified to meet residential building code — engineered into the design, not an afterthought.

Can I see the build in progress?

Your home is built under roof at our Anderson facility — progress photos are available throughout the build, and facility visits can be arranged.
