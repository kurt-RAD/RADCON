Training Structure Questions

Are your structures NFPA 1402/1403 compliant?

Our structures are designed in alignment with NFPA 1402 and 1403, developed directly with the fire training chiefs who run the evolutions, and engineered by our licensed PE. Ask us how alignment applies to your specific configuration and live-fire program.

Can we add to the structure later as budget allows?

Yes — modularity is the point. We design phase one so future phases bolt on: additional stories, props, and rooms as grants land.

What's the lead time from PO to delivery?

It depends on configuration — request a budgetary quote and we'll give you a realistic schedule for your build.

Do you install on-site?

Yes — from design through on-site assembly, one accountable team.
